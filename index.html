The game

Everyone's dice. Only your cup.

You see five dice. There are twenty-five on the table. Every bid is a claim about all of them — and every raise makes that claim a little harder to believe.

Bid on the whole table

Say how many of a face are hiding under every cup combined. You know your five. The other twenty are a read on the people you're playing with.

Ones are wild

Every 1 counts as whatever's being bid, so two 4s in view can really be four. The app paints wild pips terracotta so you never lose track — and the moment someone bids on 1s themselves, only real 1s count.

Raise it, or call it

On your turn there are two moves: push the bid higher, or call the last player a liar. No passing, no folding. The composer only offers legal raises, so all that's left to decide is whether you believe them.

−1 die

Lose a die, not the night

Cups lift, dice get counted, and whoever was wrong hands over a die. Last player holding wins. Knocked out early? The ghost seat keeps you in — call every bid bluff or legit and find out how sharp your read really is.

Questions

Asked at every table.

What is Liar's Dice?

A bluffing dice game for two to six players. Everyone rolls five dice under a cup and only sees their own. Players take turns bidding on how many dice of one face are on the whole table — "three 4s", "four 5s" — and each bid has to top the last, until someone calls the previous player a liar. Cups lift, dice get counted, and whoever was wrong loses a die. The last player with dice wins. It's the game from pirate films and westerns, also known as Dudo. The full rules are on How to play.

Is it free?

Yes — free on the App Store, with no ads and no tracking. Optional cosmetic themes, cup skins and reaction packs exist, plus a tip jar, but nothing you need to play is paid.

Can I play online with friends?

Yes. Create a private room and share its four-letter code. Up to six players join from their own iPhones or iPads, from anywhere with a connection. Each device needs to be signed in to iCloud — that's what carries the table between phones — but there's no separate account to create.

Can I play solo?

Yes. Play vs AI seats two to six players with five characters who each play differently, at three difficulties from relaxed to ruthless. The AI is probability-aware and bluffs when it should.

Can we play on one phone?

Yes. Play in Person has two modes: pass one phone around the table, with hold-to-peek keeping each hand private, or everyone uses their own phone as a private dice cup and bids out loud.

What other names does Liar's Dice go by?

Dudo — its name in Peru and Chile, where the game comes from — and Cachito. It's also the dice game you've seen played in pirate films and westerns. Whatever the table calls it, the app plays the classic rules.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 26 or later. There's no Android, Mac or Apple Watch version.
