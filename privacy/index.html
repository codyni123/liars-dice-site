Privacy Policy

Liar's Dice is designed privacy-first. No analytics SDKs, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no third-party tracking. This page explains what limited data the app does handle, and why.

TL;DR

Your dice, stats, and gameplay live on your device. Multiplayer rooms ride on Apple's iCloud so different devices can stay in sync. Sign in with Apple is optional and only used to carry your stats across your own devices. We don't run analytics, sell data, advertise, or track you.

1. Who runs this app

The Liar's Dice iOS app ("the app") is built and operated by Cody Ni, an independent developer ("we," "us"). Contact: codyni123@gmail.com.

2. What data the app handles

Stored on your device

  • Your chosen display name and avatar.
  • Your local game stats (games played, win rate, streaks, etc.).
  • App preferences (sound, haptics, appearance).

This data never leaves your device unless you opt into Sign in with Apple (below).

Multiplayer (Apple's CloudKit)

When you create or join a private multiplayer room, the app uses Apple's CloudKit service to synchronize game state — the room code, player list, current bids, and dice states — between the devices in that room. CloudKit data is associated with your Apple ID and managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have direct access to your Apple ID, name, or email through CloudKit.

Active rooms are temporary. Rooms auto-close after 5 minutes of lobby inactivity, and finished games are cleaned up automatically.

Sign in with Apple (optional)

If — and only if — you tap "Sign in with Apple" in the app's Settings, we receive an anonymous Apple-issued user identifier. We use it for one purpose: to associate your stats with your account so they sync across your own iPhone and iPad. We do not request or store your name or email through Sign in with Apple. You can stop using Sign in with Apple at any time from iOS Settings.

Crash reports (Apple's MetricKit)

The app uses Apple's MetricKit framework, which delivers aggregated, anonymous diagnostic and crash reports. These reports help us find and fix bugs. They do not include personal information.

3. What the app does NOT do

  • No third-party analytics SDKs (no Mixpanel, Amplitude, Firebase Analytics, etc.).
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad SDKs.
  • No in-app purchases or subscriptions.
  • No tracking across apps or websites — the app does not request the App Tracking Transparency permission because it has nothing to track.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of personal data. Ever.
  • No location access. No contacts access. No microphone or camera access.
  • No newsletter list, no email harvesting.

4. Data sharing

We do not share your data with third parties for advertising, analytics, or any commercial purpose. The only data leaving your device is:

  • Multiplayer game state sent to and from Apple's CloudKit, only while you're actively in a room.
  • Anonymous crash reports sent via Apple's MetricKit.
  • Your stats (if you opt into Sign in with Apple) synced via Apple's iCloud.

All three travel via Apple's infrastructure and are governed by Apple's privacy practices in addition to ours.

5. Data retention

  • Local data persists until you delete the app or clear its data in iOS Settings.
  • Multiplayer rooms are deleted automatically after they end or after 5 minutes of inactivity.
  • Sign in with Apple synced stats persist until you stop using Sign in with Apple for this app (iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple).
  • Crash reports are retained per Apple's MetricKit policy.

6. Children's privacy

The app is rated 4+ and is appropriate for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal data from anyone for advertising or analytics, this applies universally.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any data we hold — though the app stores virtually nothing server-side. Your data lives on your device and in Apple's iCloud, both of which you control directly.
  • Delete your data — uninstall the app to wipe local data; revoke Sign in with Apple to remove your synced stats.
  • Object to any processing — email us and we'll address concerns.

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have additional rights under the GDPR and CCPA. Given that the app does not run analytics or collect personal data for commercial purposes, most of those rights are satisfied by deletion (uninstall) or by managing your iCloud account directly.

8. Security

All data leaving the device travels via encrypted Apple frameworks (CloudKit over HTTPS, iCloud-managed Sign in with Apple, MetricKit). On-device data is protected by iOS's standard data-protection mechanisms.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page and, for material changes, surface a notice inside the app. We encourage you to check back occasionally.
